myAudio · Privacy Policy

myAudio processes your media on your device. No account, advertisements or analytics SDK.

1. Who we are

myAudio is an offline audio player and media toolkit provided by audio studio. This policy describes the released app. Contact: 237005722@qq.com. Effective date: 29 October 2026.

2. Media and information on your device

Files you choose, including audio, video, cover images and lyrics, are processed locally for playback, extraction, editing, enhancement and export. The app stores its managed media copies, track details, playlists, favorites, recent playback, lyric drafts and settings in its local storage. We do not operate an app account system or a server that receives these files. The app includes no advertising or analytics SDK and does not automatically send us your media or usage history.

3. Optional playback visualization

On Android, live frequency visualization may require the system audio recording permission to sample the audio being played. myAudio uses these playback samples temporarily to draw the visualization; it does not use this feature to record conversations or the surrounding microphone. You can decline permission and use the available simulated visualization instead.

4. Permissions and background work

The system file and photo pickers provide access to items you select. Older Android versions may use storage permissions for selected media. Notification and foreground service permissions support playback controls and the progress or cancellation of media processing, backups and exports. These permissions do not authorize uploading your files. Microphone hardware is optional. The release app does not require permission to draw over other apps.

5. Sharing, exports and system backups

Files leave the app when you choose to share, save or back them up through a system destination. That destination may be another app, external storage or a cloud provider you select, and its own privacy rules apply. Android may also back up or transfer app data according to your device and account settings. These operating system or user-directed transfers are different from an upload service operated by myAudio. We do not receive or control those copies.

6. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local library data and settings remain until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Temporary processing and sharing files are managed as cache. Removing a managed track does not remove your original input or copies you exported elsewhere. Delete exported files and backups at their destination separately; manage system backup copies in your device or provider settings. You can revoke permissions in Android settings. There is no myAudio account to close.

7. Security and support

App storage is protected by the operating system sandbox. Backups and exports can contain your private media and should be stored carefully. No storage method is completely secure. If you email support, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond; do not send private media unless needed for your request.

8. Intended audience

myAudio is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Contact us if you believe such information was sent to us.

9. Updates and contact

If data practices change, this policy and the app or store disclosures will be updated. The date above identifies this version. Questions or deletion requests concerning support correspondence can be sent to 237005722@qq.com.

myAudio · 隐私政策

myAudio 在设备上处理媒体，无需账号，不含广告或统计分析 SDK。

1. 我们是谁

myAudio 是 audio studio 提供的离线音频播放器与媒体工具。本政策描述已发布应用的数据处理方式。联系邮箱：237005722@qq.com。生效日期：2026年10月29日。

2. 设备上的媒体与信息

你选择的音频、视频、封面图片和歌词在本地用于播放、提取、编辑、增强与导出。应用在本地保存受管理的媒体副本、曲目信息、歌单、收藏、最近播放、歌词草稿和设置。我们不提供应用账号系统或接收这些文件的服务器；应用不含广告或统计分析 SDK，不会自动向我们发送媒体或使用历史。

3. 可选的播放频谱

Android 实时频谱可能需要系统录音权限，以采样正在播放的音频。myAudio 临时使用播放采样绘制律动，不用此功能录制对话或周围环境的麦克风声音。你可以拒绝权限，并使用可用的模拟律动效果。

4. 权限与后台任务

系统文件和照片选择器仅用于你选择的项目；旧版 Android 可能使用存储权限读取所选媒体。通知和前台服务权限用于播放控制以及媒体处理、备份和导出的进度与取消，不代表允许上传文件。麦克风硬件并非必需，正式应用不要求在其他应用上层显示。

5. 分享、导出和系统备份

当你主动分享、保存或备份时，文件可交给你选择的其他应用、外部存储或云提供方，对方适用其自身隐私规则。Android 也可能按设备和账号设置备份或迁移应用数据。这些系统行为或你主动选择的传输，不是 myAudio 提供的上传服务；我们不接收或控制这些副本。

6. 保存、删除与你的选择

本地曲库和设置会保留至你删除、清除应用存储或卸载。临时处理与分享文件作为缓存管理。删除受管理的曲目不会删除原始输入文件或此前导出的其他副本；请在相应位置另行删除导出文件和备份，并通过设备或提供方设置管理系统备份。你可随时在 Android 设置中撤销权限，无需注销 myAudio 账号，因为应用没有账号。

7. 安全与支持

应用存储受操作系统沙箱保护。备份和导出可能包含私人媒体，请妥善保存；任何存储方式都不能保证绝对安全。如果你发送支持邮件，我们会收到你主动提供的邮箱、正文与附件，并用于回复；非处理问题所必需时，请勿发送私人媒体。

8. 目标受众

myAudio 面向13岁及以上用户，不面向13岁以下儿童。我们不会主动要求儿童提供个人信息；若你认为有人向我们发送了此类信息，请联系上述邮箱。

9. 更新与联系

数据处理方式变化时，我们将更新政策以及应用或商店声明，顶部日期标识当前版本。有关本政策或支持邮件删除请求，请联系237005722@qq.com。
