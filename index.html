myAudio · Privacy Statement

Effective Date: 2026-11-05

myAudio processes your media on your device. No account, advertisements or analytics SDK.

1. Who We Are

myAudio is an offline audio player and media tool provided by 【qing cheng】(Project Name: myAudio-mobile). This privacy statement describes how this application processes information.

If you have any questions, please email us at: 237005722@qq.com.

2. What Information We Collect

myAudio is a locally processed application. We will not collect or upload your audio files, media content or recording data to any server. All audio processing is completed locally on your device.

This application requires no account registration or login. It does not track you, contain ads, or integrate any analytics or crash reporting SDKs.

Only after you actively grant permissions, the app accesses corresponding content locally:

  • Audio files from local storage (for playback, editing and export)
  • Microphone sampling (only for spectrum visualization)

Special Note: The microphone permission requested by spectrum visualization is only used to sample the currently playing audio signal. It will not record or save ambient sound, nor upload sampled data.

3. Use of Data

Media and audio data are processed, rendered and edited on your device and are not sent to external servers. Imported files and processing results can be saved on your device.

When you choose to export or share files actively, the files are passed to other applications through the system sharing component. This behavior is a system capability and is not controlled by this application. System cloud backup (such as manufacturer cloud disk) is controlled by your phone settings. This application will not trigger upload actively.

4. Permission Description

The application applies for permissions only when the corresponding function is needed:

  • Storage Permission: Read and write local audio files for importing audio and exporting processed results. You may refuse storage permission. If refused, the app cannot read local audio files, while other basic view functions that do not require file access can still work normally.
  • Microphone Permission: Only for spectrum visualization. You may refuse microphone permission. The spectrum function will be unavailable, but you can switch to simulated rhythm mode. Other playback and editing functions will not be affected.
  • Notification & Foreground Service Permission: Used to keep background audio processing and export tasks alive. If you turn off notifications, you may not see task progress. Export and processing can still continue, subject to system limits on background work.

You may revoke granted permissions at any time in your system settings. Revoking a permission may affect features that rely on it.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All audio files are stored locally on your phone. This application does not keep cloud copies of your files.

You can delete audio files anytime in the file manager or inside the app. Uninstalling this application will not automatically delete audio files you have exported and saved on your device.

6. Third-Party Services

The current version of this application does not integrate third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or user tracking. If third-party components are introduced in future versions, this privacy statement will be updated to disclose the scope and purpose of personal information processing.

This application uses open-source audio/video codec libraries for local audio decoding only. These libraries run locally and will not upload any user data over the network.

7. Minors

myAudio is intended for users aged 14 and above and is not targeted at children under 14. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If you are a guardian and find that a child has submitted information to us, please contact the email above and we will handle it accordingly.

8. Updates to this Statement

If this privacy statement changes, the effective date on this page will be updated. Your continued use of the application means you accept the updated statement.

9. Contact Us

Email: 237005722@qq.com

myAudio · 隐私声明

生效日期：2026-11-05

myAudio 在设备上处理媒体，无需账号，不含广告或统计分析 SDK。

1. 我们是谁

myAudio 是由【卿成】（项目代号：myAudio-mobile）提供的离线音频播放器与媒体工具。本隐私声明用于说明本应用处理信息的方式。

如果你有任何疑问，可以发送邮件至：237005722@qq.com。

2. 本应用收集哪些信息

myAudio 是一款以本地处理为核心的应用：我们不会收集、上传你的音频文件、媒体内容、录音数据到任何服务器。所有音频处理均在你的设备本地完成。

本应用不注册账号、不登录、不追踪你、不植入广告、不集成数据分析、崩溃统计类 SDK。

应用仅在你主动授权后，在本地读取对应内容：

  • 本地存储中的音频文件（用于播放、编辑、导出）
  • 麦克风采样（仅频谱可视化功能使用）

特别说明：频谱可视化所请求的麦克风权限，仅用于采样当前正在播放的音频信号，不会录制、保存环境声音，也不会上传采样数据。

3. 数据的使用

读取到的媒体和音频数据在你的设备本地进行运算、渲染和编辑，不会发送到外部服务器。导入的文件和处理结果可以保存在你的设备上。

你主动选择导出、分享文件时，文件由系统分享组件交给其他应用；该行为属于系统能力，不受本应用控制。系统云备份（如手机厂商云盘）由你手机系统设置控制，本应用不会主动触发上传。

4. 权限说明

本应用仅在需要对应功能时向你申请权限：

  • 存储权限：读取、写入设备本地音频文件，用于导入音频、导出处理结果。你可以拒绝授予存储权限，则应用无法读取本地音频文件；拒绝该权限后，其余不需要文件读写的基础浏览功能仍可正常使用。
  • 麦克风权限：仅用于频谱可视化。你可以拒绝麦克风权限，此时频谱功能不可用，但你可以切换使用模拟律动模式，其余音频播放、编辑功能不受影响。
  • 通知与前台服务权限：用于后台音频处理、导出任务保活。关闭通知后，你可能看不到任务进度提示；导出和处理仍可继续，后台运行受系统限制。

你可以随时在手机系统设置中撤销已经授予的权限。撤销权限可能影响依赖该权限的功能。

5. 数据留存与删除

所有音频文件都保存在你自己的手机本地，本应用不持有你的云端副本。

你可以随时在文件管理器、应用内删除音频文件；卸载本应用不会自动删除你已经导出保存到设备上的音频文件。

6. 第三方服务

本应用当前版本未集成广告、数据分析、用户追踪类第三方 SDK。如未来版本引入第三方组件，本隐私政策会同步更新披露其处理个人信息的范围与目的。

本应用使用开源音视频编解码库用于本地音频解码处理，相关库仅在本地运行，不会联网上传任何用户数据。

7. 面向未成年人

myAudio 面向 14 周岁及以上用户，不面向 14 周岁以下儿童。我们不会主动收集 14 周岁以下儿童的个人信息。如果你是监护人，发现未成年人向我们提交了信息，请联系邮箱，我们会配合处理。
